About STEM AERO

Where Curiosity Takes Flight

We are dedicated to unlocking every young student's creative and analytical potential through experiential robotics, electronics, and artificial intelligence.

Our Journey

Bridging the Gap Between Classroom Theory and Real Engineering

STEM AERO was born out of a clear realization: in a rapidly digitizing world, Indian school students were learning about technology from static textbooks, but rarely had the tools to touch, build, and program it themselves.

Traditional computer labs taught word processing and slide presentations, while the world was racing toward autonomous vehicles, IoT automation, and neural networks. On the other hand, many schools invested in expensive robotics kits that ended up locked in cupboards because teachers lacked continuous training and dedicated lesson plans.

STEM AERO changed that dynamic. We created a complete, turnkey academic ecosystem: certified full-time trainers placed directly in school campuses, supported by a 10-year progressive curriculum, and backed by 100% child-safe, low-voltage hardware laboratories.

In-School Robotics Lab
Hands-On Mentorship in Action: Certified trainers guiding students through autonomous robotics, circuit wiring, and programming.

Our Mission

To democratize world-class STEM, Robotics, and Artificial Intelligence education across K-12 schools by delivering affordable, turnkey lab ecosystems, rigorously trained mentors, and an engaging 10-year progressive syllabus aligned with NEP 2020.

Our Vision

To build a generation of confident Indian problem-solvers, inventors, and researchers who do not simply consume foreign technology, but possess the foundational mastery to innovate and lead the next era of global aerospace, AI, and robotics.

Safety by Design

Our Uncompromising Safety Commitment

Parental peace of mind and student safety are the non-negotiable foundation of every single STEM AERO kit, lab, and workshop.

Low-Voltage Systems

All student kits operate exclusively on ultra-safe 5V to 9V DC. Absolutely zero high-voltage mains exposure at student workbenches.

Teacher-Approved Power

Power supplies include built-in automatic thermal shutoff, reverse-polarity guards, and short-circuit protection circuits.

Lead-Free & Modular

Components use child-safe jumper wires and plug-and-play headers. Zero hazardous soldering required for regular curriculum classes.

Trained Safety Protocols

Every student is trained in tidy bench management, tool ergonomics, and proper battery handling from their very first day.
