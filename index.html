Proven Pedagogy

The 6-Phase Experiential Teaching Cycle

Every single weekly session moves through an intentional instructional sequence designed to turn passive listeners into active inventors.

1

Concept

Interactive real-world hook unpacking the physics or computing law.

2

Demonstrate

Trainer demonstrates working hardware benchmark & test cases.

3

Build

Students assemble physical circuits, sensors, and mechanical gears.

4

Code

Students write logic blocks or code routines to drive actuators.

5

Challenge

Open-ended bug or scenario forcing creative troubleshooting.

6

Reflect

Journal documentation, peer demos, and engineering takeaway.

Scientifically balanced classroom distribution for maximum retention and creative problem-solving.

30% Theory
50% Hands-On
20% Project
