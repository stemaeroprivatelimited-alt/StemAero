Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions by Schools

Quick answers on deployment schedules, trainer qualifications, and budget plans.

How quickly can STEM AERO deploy trainers to our school?

Following our mutual agreement and timetable alignment, dedicated trainers can be stationed on your campus within 7 to 10 working days.

Do we need an existing lab room to start?

Not necessarily. Our trainers can conduct sessions in standard science/computer rooms with our portable, self-contained modular hardware kits, or we can assist you in converting any standard room into a full ATL-style lab.

Are the kits completely safe for younger children in Class 3 and 4?

Absolutely. All kits operate on safe low-voltage 5V DC with short-circuit and overcurrent safeguards. No mains power, soldering, or sharp cutting tools are ever used in student sessions.
