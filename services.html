Core Offering 2

10-Year Progressive Curriculum (Classes 3 to 12)

Unlike disjointed summer camps or one-off robotics kits, STEM AERO provides a unified, continuous academic syllabus. As students grow from elementary school to high school, our curriculum elevates their skills systematically from visual blocks to production-grade AI code.

120 Graded Chapters: Exactly 12 structured chapters per class level, ensuring clear pacing and milestone completion each term.
Hands-On Ratio (50% Building / 20% Projects): 70% of total class time is spent actively constructing physical mechanisms and writing code.
Hackathons & Competition Mentorship: Direct coaching for national robotics olympiads, Atal Tinkering challenges, and international science expos.
Aligned with NEP 2020: Fully compliant with National Education Policy mandates for experiential, inquiry-based STEM learning.

Turnkey AI & Robotics Lab Setup (ATL-Style Labs)
